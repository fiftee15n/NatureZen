

# 🌿 NatureZen

NatureZen is a comprehensive platform dedicated to plant enthusiasts. Explore a wide range of plants, access detailed care guides, and enhance your green space with our curated accessories.

## 🚀 Features

- **Diverse Plant Collection:** Browse through various categories, including indoor plants, DIY gardening, and more.
- **Informative Guides:** Access detailed information on plant care, gardening tips, and DIY projects.
- **Quality Accessories:** Discover a selection of gardening tools and accessories to complement your plant collection.


## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
- **Design:** Responsive layouts with a focus on user experience
- **Media:** High-quality images and videos for an immersive experience
